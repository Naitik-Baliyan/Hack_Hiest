ABOUT HACKHEIST

HackHeist is the flagship developer-first event organized by GDG on Campus MIET — an arena designed for visionary coders, designers, and problem solvers to push boundaries, learn fast, and build extraordinary solutions.

Whether you are pitching an inventive prototype, refining practical engineering workflows, or engaging with seasoned industry mentors, HackHeist brings the developer community together under one roof. Escape the ordinary, challenge conventions, and turn bold ideas into high-impact products.
